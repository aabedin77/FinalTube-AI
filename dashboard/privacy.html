Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective Date: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FinalTube AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and channel credentials you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy explains how FinalTube AI collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your YouTube channel and use our automated growth intelligence and video production platform.

By connecting your YouTube channel or accessing FinalTube AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. YouTube OAuth Integration & Permissions

FinalTube AI integrates with Google OAuth 2.0 services to interact with your YouTube channel. When you click "Connect with YouTube", you grant FinalTube AI explicit permission to access specific YouTube scopes necessary for automated channel management:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
    YouTube Direct Upload Permission: Allows FinalTube AI to upload completed 1080p MP4 videos, set custom thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, and publishing visibility status (Public, Private, or Unlisted) based on your configured channel preferences.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
    YouTube Read-Only Profile Permission: Allows FinalTube AI to retrieve your YouTube channel profile name, avatar image, upload confirmation status, and video list.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
    YouTube Analytics Read-Only Permission: Allows FinalTube AI to collect aggregate performance metrics (views, watch time, subscribers gained, CTR) to generate AI content strategy recommendations and improve future video topic selection.

FinalTube AI complies strictly with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do NOT request permissions beyond what is required to generate, publish, and analyze your channel's content.

3. Use of Generative AI Services

To produce high-quality long-form videos, scripts, thumbnails, and narration, FinalTube AI interacts with external generative AI model providers:

  • Google Gemini API (@google/genai): Used for researching trending What-If topics, generating Hindi scripts, structuring scene breakdowns, and synthesizing natural Hindi audio narration.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI FLUX Models: Used for generating scene-specific 2D cinematic illustrations and custom thumbnail graphics.

Data Privacy in AI Processing: Prompt text and visual topic prompts sent to Gemini and Cloudflare AI contain non-personal, topic-focused creative content (e.g. "What if Earth stops rotating?"). No personal user data or OAuth credentials are ever shared with or submitted to AI model providers for training purposes.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We believe in data minimization and secure local storage:

  • OAuth Tokens: Google OAuth tokens (access tokens and long-lived refresh tokens) are stored securely on the application server in restricted, private configuration storage. Tokens are never exposed in public client logs or client-side JavaScript.
  • Channel Preferences & Strategy: Your selected niche, target audience, video format, duration, and publishing preferences are stored in the application database (`youtube_automation.db`) to enable single-setup automated publishing.
  • Retention: Your credentials and preferences are retained only for as long as your channel remains connected to FinalTube AI.

5. Disconnecting & Revoking Access

You maintain full control over your YouTube channel and credentials at all times:

  • Disconnecting via Dashboard: You can disconnect your YouTube channel at any time by navigating to Settings → YouTube Connection → Disconnect. Disconnecting immediately removes and deletes your stored OAuth tokens from our server.
  • Revoking Access via Google: You can also revoke FinalTube AI's access to your Google account at any time directly through Google Account Third-Party Security Settings. Revoking access instantly invalidates all stored tokens.

6. Third-Party Links & Terms

FinalTube AI utilizes YouTube API Services. By using FinalTube AI to publish videos, users are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
